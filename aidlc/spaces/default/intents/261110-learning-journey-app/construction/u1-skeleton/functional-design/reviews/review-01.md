### Summary

The model is mostly consistent with C1, C3, C4 and C5 and with the story criteria. Three gaps remain: the log evidence required by AC1.3.3, the user-visible throttle message required by AC9.2.1, and the slot release on the save-failure paths at the U3 boundary. Each needs a small, concrete addition before a developer can build without guessing.
