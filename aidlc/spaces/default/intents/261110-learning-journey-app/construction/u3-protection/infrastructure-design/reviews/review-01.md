### Summary

The ingress, IAM, TTL, rollback and metric-filter claims check out against the built stack and code. Three design gaps remain: the guard's conditional update is not specified well enough to create the item safely, the smoke checks contradict each other because the cap cannot be reset the same day, and a guard that fails open has no metric or alarm even though the stated reason it needs none is false.
