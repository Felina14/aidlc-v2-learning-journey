### Summary

The design covers the acceptance criteria for US7.1, US7.2, AC9.2.2 and AC9.2.3. The DynamoDB guard design (a conditional increment on a per-session-per-day item sharing the partition, with a sort key of USAGE#date) is achievable. There are two Major gaps: the guard-side release dedupe contradicts contract C4 and cannot be built from the stored data, and the behaviour of the way back when the journey is gone is undefined. Both can be fixed by clarification, so the verdict is READY (zero Critical, two Major).
