### Summary

The design is implementable: criteria are covered, the goal call is one conditional update in both forms, the date and pace maths hold for the 28-day, 2-day, due-today, passed and all-done cases, and the style checks fit the existing list limits. Two Major gaps should be closed before code: the "saved" announcement before the answer, and a goal change in flight when the journey is deleted or replaced. The Minor items tighten edge cases.
