### Summary

The infrastructure claims about IAM, the new route, CloudFront, the throttle, the alarm, the metric filter and the rollback all check out against the stack and the built code. One open Major concern: the longer instructor and planner replies are never weighed against the 4096-token output limit and the 12 s per-call limit, so a cut-off reply would quietly turn into a template. With one Major and three Minor findings the design is implementable, and the Major should be settled in Code Generation or by amending the design.
