### Summary

The work is sound. The tests assert real things: the real controller is driven, axe is shown to catch violations, the contrast and luminance math is correct, and the four fixes are small and match the spec rather than adding features. The record is honest about the manual items: every one is `not run`, and AC8.2.4 and AC8.3.2 are open. Two Major gaps are left to fix: 13 of the 36 states are not actually verified as reached while the documents say they are, and traceability.json marks AC8.3.3 (and BR50.2, BR50.6) as `OK` when they depend on manual passes that have not been run. With no Critical and two Major findings, the verdict is READY under the review rules, but both Major items should be fixed before the stage is called complete.
