### Summary

The design is honest about the human-only checks and holds to "no new features". Two Major findings must be settled in Code Generation: the contrast table must match the real stylesheet, and the live-region rule must be reconciled with the page as built. With no Critical and two Major findings, a developer can build from it. The Minor items tighten what jsdom can claim and what counts as done for AC8.2.4 and AC8.3.2.
