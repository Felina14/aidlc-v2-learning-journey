# Stakeholder Map — Learning Journey App

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Primary Interest | Authority | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Learner (end user) | Primary user of the app | Gets a clear, staged, actionable learning journey for any professional topic they choose; sees their progress and stays motivated | None over scope or build decisions | [desc] |
| Builder / Product Owner | Designer, developer, and decision-maker for this build | Clean, maintainable architecture; lowest reasonable AWS cost; easy to tear down when the demo is done | Full — sole scope and priority decision-maker | [Q5][Q6] |
| Demo audience | Potential users, interviewers, or colleagues who will evaluate the working app | A coherent, polished demonstration of the app's end-to-end value; confidence in the technical choices (Bedrock, serverless AWS) | Influential but not authoritative — feedback informs but doesn't override the builder | [Q5] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

| Name / Role | Type | What They Decide or Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Builder / Product Owner | Decision-maker | All scope, priority, and architecture decisions for this build | [Q6] |
| Demo audience | Influencer | May surface usability or feature feedback after seeing the working app; no formal approval required | [Q5] |

## Communication Requirements

There is no formal reporting cadence or communication requirement for this build. The builder ships when the app is ready, with no scheduled reviews or sign-offs required. [Q6]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- [assumption] The "demo audience" is not formally identified by role or name at this stage; it represents whoever the builder chooses to show the app to. No structured feedback collection or review process is planned.
- [assumption] No external sponsor, manager, or funding body has a stake in this build. This is a solo, self-funded initiative.
